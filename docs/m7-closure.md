## What the milestone establishes

Pressure inadequacy persists across the tested designs and new regulation forms. In T7.2, no sampled case reaches the declared final-window 65 mmHg MAP screen; the range is approximately 39.5–57.8 mmHg. New stronger cerebral-regulation alternatives increase brain flow but decrease MAP and increase auxiliary deficit. A favorable brain-flow change alone cannot certify whole-system adequacy.

Short metabolic deficit predictions are more equation-dependent. The extraction-form experiment changes nominal deficit by about 72.5%; finite reserve accounting can cover much of a short deficit under extra assumptions. Neither changes the need for adequate pressure or proves available oxygen, metabolic-to-force coupling, injury avoidance or reversibility.

Native baseline matching does not identify arbitrary model inputs. MAP/CO constrain combinations; regional allocation, inactive auxiliary design and hydraulically disconnected metabolic parameters need other evidence. The selected four-parameter native subset is only locally and conditionally recoverable with richer observations and fixed nuisance parameters.

**291 tests pass.** New comparisons retain paired numerical checks, conserved ledgers and failure records. The initially inadequate reserve integration pair is preserved and replaced with a tighter passing pair. Prior evidence remains hash-verified. Scientific reports, protocols and results are linked above; `reports/m7-final/manifest.json` freezes the final evidence.

## Conditions carried into M8

1. Separate design variables from uncertain physiology and model-form alternatives. Define objectives and constraints before optimization; preserve infeasible cases.
2. Use independent pressure, regional-delivery and pump self-supply checks. Do not reward reduced actual consumption after unmet demand as an efficiency gain.
3. Compare promising candidates across relevant structural assumptions. A nominal optimum is not robustness; joint structural/parameter coverage remains limited.
4. Use finite stock accounting and horizon dependence. Ten-second stock insensitivity does not imply long-duration fuel adequacy.
5. Treat native parameter fits as conditional. Do not claim physiological uniqueness, population probabilities or calibrated survival intervals.
6. Keep R-M3-01 (source thoracic-pressure response), L-M4-F01 (instantaneous substrate/baseline limitations), prescribed BIO contraction, uncertain demand states, regional anatomy, CO2/pH, injury/reperfusion and thrombosis visible. Supply-limited contraction and full tissue buffering are not implemented; optimization cannot establish biological feasibility or a repair window without addressing those dependencies.

These conditions satisfy the M7 gate: dominant assumptions, unstable rankings, sampling adequacy and identifiability caveats are explicit, and scenario fractions are not presented as patient survival probabilities. M8 should begin with its own frozen experiment/design-optimization protocol. No manuscript drafting or publication action is started by this closure.
