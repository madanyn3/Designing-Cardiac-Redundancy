At reference the coupled 60 s replay sustained zero unmet demand in all three organ groups and measured hepatic export at **0.105 mmol/min**, which is the declared release cap of 0.21 multiplied by the liver-function fraction of 0.50. The simulated drain matches the analytic one at that effective rate.

The export **rate** binds, not the store. The accessible glycogen store is 329.8 mmol, which at the effective export rate would last **3141 minutes** — two orders of magnitude beyond the 23.6 mmol circulating pool that actually runs out. An earlier statement of this comparison quoted the store term as "19,786", which is the store in mmol multiplied by 60 and carries no dimension; the properly dimensioned figures are given here.

The floor is a declared reporting axis the model does not set, and it moves the answer by a factor of three. Sources: `reports/m8-duration/duration-scenarios.json` (scenario rows, computed with the liver-function fraction applied) and `reports/m8-duration/verification-60s.json` (the measured 60 s replay). The analytic block of the latter reports 39.57 min at reference because it omits that fraction; 33.8 min supersedes it.
